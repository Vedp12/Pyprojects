Let's analyze World Cup odds around Ivory Coast vs. Norway and make our expert soccer picks and predictions.Ivory Coast, No. 31 in the FIFA World Rankings, clinched a spot in the World Cup by winning Group F in the African qualifiers. It was given a solid draw in Group E and opened play with a 1-0 win over Ecuador. It then met its match against Germany, losing 2-1, before bouncing back with a 2-0 win over Curaçao. F Nicolas Pepe, who scored both goals against Curaçao and also captains the team, is the main weapon for Ivory Coast.Norway, ranked 23rd, had a strong Group I showing. It throttled Iraq 4-1 in its first game and took down Senegal 3-2 in its second performance. Against France, which was also 2-0-0 at that point, Norway lost 4-1. F Erling Haaland has scored a team-high 4 goals through the 3 group stage matches.
