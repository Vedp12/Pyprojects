Within the garden, visitors will find a number of wonderous hidden gems and breathtaking venues.The Helis Foundation Enrique Alférez Sculpture Garden features artworks from Mexican American New Orleans artist Enrique Alférez. Sweeping footpaths take you through the 8,000-square-foot garden that features 15 sculptures and is surrounded by sprawling greenery and oak trees.The Conservatory of Two Sisters, originally constructed in the 1930s, is a state-of-the-art showcase of flora, with two exhibits including Living Fossils and Tropical Rainforest.Also within the botanical garden is the Garden Study Center, which includes a unique venue space and a whimsical lily pond.
